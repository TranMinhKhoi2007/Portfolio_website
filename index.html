Building My Foundation in Cybersecurity

Hello! I am a second-year Information Technology student with a growing passion for cybersecurity and computer systems. My journey began with a genuine curiosity about how software, operating systems, and networks operate beneath the surface, which naturally evolved into a focus on how systems can be protected and made resilient against vulnerabilities.

Currently, I am actively building my foundational knowledge across essential areas of cybersecurity. I focus on computer networking protocols, Linux environments, operating system internals, security fundamentals, and structured programming principles in C and C++.

Alongside my university coursework, I dedicate time to self-study, practical lab exercises, and hands-on programming projects. I strongly believe in learning by doing—analyzing system behaviors, practicing problem-solving, and writing clean, disciplined code to understand how vulnerabilities emerge and how to prevent them.

My goal is to continuously strengthen my technical foundation and practical capabilities. Looking forward, I am eager to pursue an internship or entry-level opportunity in cybersecurity where I can learn from experienced professionals, tackle real-world challenges, and contribute effectively.
